## Coverage and method

All 26 active Starting Point items were compared with all 82 active Check items: Gear 10, Pulley 10, Lever 10, Mixed Mechanical 16, Numerical 12, Abstract/Logical 12, Verbal 12. Within-domain comparison coverage is 8×46 Mechanical plus 6×12 for each other domain = **584 pairs**. Cross-domain pairs have no plausible matching learner problem. All 108 current source records are appended below. Source specifications for ID-keyed Mechanical diagrams were considered; their code is unchanged. No calibration, inactive bank or Guided/Independent item was treated as a Check substitute.

Classification: (1) identical complete learner problem, (2) materially near-identical stimulus/configuration/task where recognition could substitute for solving, (3) same archetype/competency requiring fresh application, (4) no meaningful counterpart. Sharing a physical law or reasoning skill alone is not duplication. A changed number alone is not sufficient novelty. The evidence column explains the new operation, rule, task or logical commitment where it changes. These are reasoned judgments, not an automated semantic guarantee.

## Acceptance and retained borderline cases

**No identical complete learner problems were found after the replacements. The 13 specifically authorized direct/near-duplicate problems have been replaced with new tasks.** The conservative prior flag for `START-NUM-006` ↔ `NUM-A-001` remains unresolved: the amendment explicitly requires that Starting Point item and the Check to remain unchanged. It is not appropriate to silently reclassify the prior table-template concern solely to claim an unconditional pass, or to modify a fourteenth item.

The exact retained tables are: Starting North 36/45=80%, South 45/50=90%, East 34/40=85%, West 48/60=80%; Check A 42/50=84%, B 54/72=75%, C 38/40=95%, D 63/75=84%. A learner must compute a new ranking and cannot simply recall the earlier winner. This supports a defensible same-archetype interpretation; identical wording/layout and rehearing the exact comparison routine support the earlier conservative near-template interpretation. Product-owner review should decide whether this known retained case satisfies the study standard. No fourteen-item substitution was attempted.

Additional retained borderline similarities, explicitly reported rather than rewritten: `START-MECH-002` ↔ `MMA-HYD-001` (same equal-pressure/area-force proposition; why versus numerical ratio), and `START-NUM-004` ↔ `NUM-A-010` (same foundational ratio-share template; distinct inputs/answer). Their retention is explicit in the amendment. They are classified here as same competency/template with a need for fresh application, while preserving the prior report’s concerns.

The new `START-MECH-003` necessarily retains a direct external gear pair because that is the specified concept. Its inverse consistency task replaces known-driver/output prediction; the downstream item still requires identifying the correct direction for its own driver/configuration. Learning “external meshes oppose rotation” is intended transfer, not remembering a particular earlier answer. Similar defining concepts recur for fixed-pulley, lever moment balance and conditional-instruction items; novelty is in task/configuration rather than changing the skill.

**Review disposition:** exact 13-item scope complete; strict global acceptance remains qualified by the explicitly retained `START-NUM-006` case. Stop for product-owner review. No further rewrites are authorized or made here.
